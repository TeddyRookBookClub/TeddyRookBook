Your name in a cartouche

A modern game, played with real signs. Egyptians wrote only royal names in cartouches, and they wrote mostly consonants. When scribes of the Ptolemaic and Roman periods had to write Greek and Latin names (Ptolemy, Cleopatra, Caesar), they spelled them with sound signs, as this tool does. So there is no single right way to write your name.

Letters A to Z; up to 16 letters. Accents are dropped.

Read a real cartouche

The names of real rulers as they appear in their cartouches. Watch for the sun disk 𓇳 of the god Ra: it is written first out of respect but often read last.
