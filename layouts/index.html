What is this?

Teddy Rook Book is a free, one-person project for learning at any age: a full school day from classic schoolbooks, math from counting to college, Greek and Latin, science you can play with, life skills and the trades, business courses, and the great books. There’s nothing to join or buy, with no accounts, ads or sign-ups. Use whatever helps, whether you teach your own children, run a class, or just like to learn.

  • 🧮 Homeschool A full school day from free public-domain schoolbooks, with a step-by-step guide for teaching each lesson: reading with McGuffey’s Readers, spelling, grammar and writing, Ray’s Arithmetic and geometry, science, nature study, history, geography, Latin and Greek. Plus Hannah Ward’s early-years curricula, sign language, a placement check, a lesson planner, practice sheets and the old eighth-grade exams to aim for.
  • 🪜 Math Ladder Math from counting in Kindergarten through college: calculus 1–4, linear algebra, differential equations, discrete math, probability, number theory, complex analysis, numerical analysis, real analysis and abstract algebra. 1,305 short topics, each with a lesson built from first principles, worked examples, pictures for the parts that trip people up, and practice that checks every answer. A placement check finds where to start, and spaced review brings each topic back before it fades. Progress stays in your browser.
  • 🪐 More ladders The same lessons, practice and review for science, economics, logic, etiquette, Egyptology, ancient scripts and vocabulary, with one shared progress: the Physics Ladder (physical science through astrophysics, 448 topics), the Chemistry Ladder (high school through general chemistry, 219), the Earth & Space Ladder (earth science, weather and climate, and geology, 180) the Economics Ladder (micro, macro and intermediate micro, 186), the Logic Ladder (informal, traditional and symbolic logic, 156), the Etiquette Ladder (manners from age 4 to adult, 148) and the Egyptology Ladder (ancient Egypt's history, hieroglyphs, religion, art and archaeology, 306), with an Ancient Egypt map and timeline, the Ancient Scripts Ladder (Linear A and Linear B, 53) and the Vocabulary Ladder (1,000 illustrated words and the Greek and Latin roots, 140). When a topic needs math you haven’t met, the Math Ladder teaches it first.
  • 🗣️ Languages Learn Koine Greek and Latin from the Gospels and the classics: listening drills, flashcards, a reader that explains every word, and a free study companion with 124 New Testament passages and every form of the 345 most common words of each language.
  • ⚛️ Science Lab Simulations built on real physics, chemistry and biology, from magnetic bottles for fusion to orbits and rockets, a trebuchet, a cart balancing a swinging chain of books, pendulums, circuits, light, gases, acids, atoms, the sky, and predators and prey, each with missions and a lesson with the equations. Plus whole units on Newton’s laws of motion and the laws of thermodynamics.
  • 🎹 Music Piano for someone who has never touched one, how to read music with drills for every clef and rhythm, 20 public-domain pieces from Hot Cross Buns to the Moonlight Sonata with the score and the keys lighting up together as they play, a metronome, and a two-hand rhythm trainer for patterns like three against two. Now also guitar, ukulele and banjo from the first chord, with songs in tab, a tuner that listens through your microphone, a chord finder and a left-handed view.
  • 📚 Harvard Classics Daily A reading for every day of the year from Dr. Eliot's Five Foot Shelf, as the 1930 reading guide lays it out: Eliot's short introduction and the full text, about fifteen minutes each, with a calendar and today's reading here on the home page.
  • 📝 Test Prep Timed SAT, ACT and CLT math practice in each test's own format, for today's versions and the ones before them, with what changed and what the evidence says about whether they got easier, grammar practice, and an AP map. Plus brain puzzles to print with answer keys, and a cube solver.
  • 🏛️ Games Browser games set in ancient Greece and Rome that teach Greek and Latin as you play, the board games of antiquity, and a daily word puzzle.
  • 🖼️ Infographics One-page visual guides: character maps, reading guides, and explainers on faith, science and finance. Open one on a screen or print it for your group.
  • 🎬 Videos Walk-throughs of the infographics and the ideas behind them, and long read-alongs like Greek and Latin with Jerome, each with a short trailer where there is one.
  • 🔧 Being an Adult 101 Step-by-step guides to what every adult should know, from resetting a breaker and changing a tire to reading a paycheck, filing taxes and cooking safely, each with the science or math behind it. For the next step, The Trades teach how things work and how to fix them, with full courses on cars, plumbing, electrical, HVAC and construction.
  • 🥕 Grow and make A food garden planner for any space, with guides for chickens and bees, and step-by-step sewing and knitting.
  • 💼 Business Short cases from real companies, each told briefly with an MBA-level takeaway, full courses in econometrics and game theory with live simulations, your tax receipt, plus market charts, economic data and finance calculators.

For readers: tens of thousands of free ebooks and audiobooks, reading paths through the great books, and a timeline of the ancient world linked to the books, games and infographics here. Also here: my home library catalog and the markets dashboards I use myself. They’re mostly for me, but they’re public, so use them however you like. There’s also a page of free resources elsewhere, from homeschool curricula and science to gardening, Latin, Greek and the great books.
