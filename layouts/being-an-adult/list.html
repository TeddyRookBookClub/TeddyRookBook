Being an Adult 101

The things a good parent teaches before a kid leaves home: fixing what breaks around the house, looking after a car, handling money and taxes, cooking safely, and the first grown-up steps like renting a place and registering to vote.

Plenty of adults can tell you that the mitochondria is the powerhouse of the cell but have never changed a tire or filed a tax return. Both kinds of knowledge matter, and they connect: a tripped breaker is a lesson in watts and amps, a car jack is a lever, and a credit card balance is compound interest working against you.

Each of these {{ $n }} guides gives the tools, the steps, the common mistakes, when to call a pro, and the science or math behind it, with a suggestion of when a child can watch, help, and do it alone. Many have a small calculator, and every one prints as a one-page checklist. The steps come from U.S. government, university extension and other reliable sources, listed on every page.
