🎀 Cookie swap planner

At a cookie swap, everyone bakes one kind of cookie in quantity and goes home with a mix of all of them. Tell the planner how many people are swapping and how many cookies each person takes from each baker.

Pick a sturdy cookieShortbread, snickerdoodles, gingerbread, crinkles and fudge travel well. Delicate iced cookies need a flat box, one layer deep.
Bake aheadMost doughs freeze for 3 months. Bake a batch an evening over the week before, or freeze baked cookies and thaw them in their container.
Label every boxWrite the cookie’s name and what it contains (wheat, milk, eggs, nuts, peanuts, soy), so guests with allergies can choose safely.
Share the recipePrint the recipe card from the recipe page and tuck one in each box.
