Magnetic Bottles for Fusion

To get energy from fusion, you have to hold a gas hotter than 100 million degrees without letting it touch anything. The bottle is a twisted doughnut of magnetic field. Turn, slice and mirror-test two exact ones from a 2026 paper, and see why the field lines must twist.
