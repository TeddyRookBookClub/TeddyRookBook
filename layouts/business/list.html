For families and students

Many homeschooling parents also run a business. The lessons and questions are written at the level of a business-school class, so the cases suit older students and adults: read one a week and work through the discussion questions together, deciding what you would have done. They pair well with the arithmetic of percentage, interest and profit in Ray's Practical Arithmetic.
