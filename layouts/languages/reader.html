❓ How the reader works
  1. Pick a Gospel and chapter at the top, or use ‹ › to turn chapters.
  2. Greek / Latin / English switch the columns. English (ESV) appears only for the 499 drill verses, marked ★. ★ only hides the other verses.
  3. Hover (or tap on a phone) any Greek or Latin word for a quick meaning; click it for the full breakdown in the Word study panel.
  4. Glosses puts a short meaning under every word, like an interlinear Bible.
  5. ▶ after a verse reads it aloud. ★ verses use the recorded Greek; the rest use your device’s voices. Speed sets the pace.
  6. Red letters mark the words of Jesus. In Greek and Latin the red is matched from the English and can be off by a word or two at the start or end of a quote.
  7. Verse numbers sometimes differ slightly between the Greek, the Latin Vulgate and the ESV; each column shows its own text for that number.
