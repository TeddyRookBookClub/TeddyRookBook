Hieroglyph flashcards

Start with the alphabet of one-sound signs, then add the two- and three-sound signs, the determinatives and real words. Say the answer to yourself, flip the card and rate how well you knew it; each card comes back just before you would forget it.

❓ How the flashcards work
  1. Pick a deck below. The alphabet is the place to start: the 25 one-sound signs in the order Egyptologists list them.
  2. Say the answer before you flip. Trying to remember, even when you can’t, is what makes a sign stick.
  3. Grade each card. Again brings it back in a minute; Hard, Good and Easy send it further out (each button shows when you’ll see it next). This is spaced repetition: cards you know come back after days, then weeks.
  4. Which side first: Hieroglyph first shows the sign or word and asks for its sound or meaning; Sound or meaning first asks you to recall the sign; Both makes two cards of each.
  5. Sounds are shown in transliteration with the way Egyptologists say them (“nefer” for nfr) and the Manuel de Codage typing form. Nobody knows exactly how the vowels sounded.
  6. Keys: Space or Enter flips; 1–4 grade (Again, Hard, Good, Easy); S skips.
