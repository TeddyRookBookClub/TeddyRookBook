❓ How the flashcards work
  1. Pick a deck below. The vowels is the place to start; then take the rows in any order (the d, k, n, p and t rows hold many common words).
  2. Say the answer before you flip. Trying to remember, even when you can’t, is what makes a sign stick.
  3. Grade each card. Again brings it back in a minute; Hard, Good and Easy send it further out (each button shows when you’ll see it next).
  4. Which side first: Sign first shows the sign and asks for its value; Value first asks you to recall the sign; Both makes two cards of each.
  5. Values are written the way scholars transliterate them: ka, pu2. Remember that r also stands for l, and p for b and ph.
  6. Keys: Space or Enter flips; 1–4 grade (Again, Hard, Good, Easy); S skips.
