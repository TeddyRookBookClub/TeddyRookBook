❓ How the drills work
  1. Press Play. Each sentence plays in the order shown under Languages & order. By default that's Greek, then English, then Latin. Use ▲▼ to reorder.
  2. Text / Audio switches choose, per language, whether you see it, hear it, both, or neither. Turn off English text to test yourself.
  3. Say it back. Repeat the Greek or Latin out loud during the pause. Pause between sentences sets that gap (1–5 s), and Repeat plays the Greek and Latin up to three times.
  4. Speed runs from 0.5× to 3.5×. It applies to all audio.
  5. Pause to study. While paused, tap any Greek or Latin word. The Word study panel shows its dictionary form, meaning, case/tense/mood and job in the sentence. Tick Word-by-word glosses to see a meaning under every word.
  6. ▶ next to a line plays just that language. ⏮ ⏭ move between sentences. On a keyboard: Space pauses, ← → go back and forward.
  7. What to practice narrows the sentences to one Gospel or one passage, in shuffle or in order.
  8. Red letters mark the words of Jesus. Untick the box to turn them off.
  9. Progress counts sentences in this visit and in total. It is saved only in this browser.
  10. About the audio: Greek is a human reading (Marilyn Phemister); Latin and English use your device’s built-in voices, so they sound different on each device. If you hear nothing, check that your device isn’t muted and try Safari or Chrome.
