Flashcards

Learn the alphabet of one-sound signs, then the common two- and three-sound signs, the determinatives, the numbers and real words. Spaced review brings each card back just before you’d forget it.

Cartouche Maker

Type a name and see it in sound signs, choose the sign for each sound, set it in a cartouche across or down, then print it or save it as a picture. Then try reading the cartouches of real pharaohs.

Typing Helper

Type transliteration with an ordinary keyboard, the way Egyptologists do (Htp → ḥtp), and copy the result. It works the other way too, and shows how each word is said.

Where to start

  • New to hieroglyphs? Read how they work (ten minutes), then learn the alphabet on flashcards, a few signs a day.
  • Ages 8 to 12: the Young Explorers course has a unit on scribes and hieroglyphs, and the cartouche maker is a fun way to practice the sound signs.
  • Studying Middle Egyptian: follow Middle Egyptian I and II, keep the sign list open beside you, and use the typing helper for your notes.
  • Curious about the history? See Ancient Egypt: the map, the timeline and the courses on history, religion and archaeology.
