How learner profiles work
  • Learners live in this browser only. There are no accounts, and nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Each learner has their own progress: the ladders, lesson ticks, phonics, flashcards, words, games and tools. Text size, units, light or dark, the lesson planner and the meal and garden plans are shared by everyone on this device.
  • To use a learner on another phone or computer, save them to a file (or copy a save code) and open it there.
  • Clearing this browser’s data (history, cookies and site data) removes every learner. A file keeps a copy safe.
  • In the lesson planner, link each child to a learner to see that learner’s work for today and their ladder progress.
