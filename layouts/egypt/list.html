Ancient Egypt

Three thousand years of kings, temples and tombs along one river, and a script no one could read for fourteen centuries until it was cracked in 1822. Here you can learn it the way a good college program teaches it: the history and how its dates are known, the gods, temples and art, the archaeology, and Middle Egyptian, the language of the hieroglyphs. There's a first course for young explorers, too.

Where to start

  • Ages 8 to 12: Ancient Egypt for Young Explorers, read together with a grown-up or on your own.
  • High school: History of Ancient Egypt, then Religion, Art and Architecture.
  • To read hieroglyphs: Middle Egyptian I starts from the first sign; no history needed.
  • Not sure? Find your rung with a short placement check.
