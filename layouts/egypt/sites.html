How the inscriptions are shown

Each inscription is drawn in TRB Hieroglyphs, the site's own hieroglyph font, with royal names in their cartouches. Under it are the transliteration, the Egyptian sounds in the Egyptologists' alphabet, and a translation, and you can open a sign-by-sign table. The texts are short and their readings standard; each card names its source. Where a text's signs or layout can't be shown faithfully yet, the card gives the reading only and says why. To learn to read them yourself, start with Middle Egyptian I, or try reading a cartouche in the Young Explorers course.
