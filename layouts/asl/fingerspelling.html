Fingerspelling practice

The 26 handshapes of the ASL alphabet, a reading game that shows a word one handshape at a time, and words to spell yourself.

Tap a letter to see it large.

Press “New word” to start.

Fingerspell this word to someone, then press “Show me” to check your handshapes.

Type a name or word to see it in handshapes.

Good to know

  • Make a printable fingerspelling puzzle, alone or mixed with English, Latin, Morse or binary, on the Mix-and-Match Worksheets page.
  • The drawings show a right-handed signer as you see them, facing you. Left-handed signers use the mirror image.
  • J and Z move: J traces a hook with the little finger, and Z draws a Z in the air with the index finger. The arrows show the motion.
  • Real fingerspelling flows from one letter to the next and is read as a whole shape, not letter by letter. To see it moving, watch a fluent signer, for example in Lifeprint's lessons ↗.
