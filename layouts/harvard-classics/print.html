Laying out the readings…

In the print window, choose Save as PDF (or Microsoft Print to PDF) as the printer. Under More settings, turn off Headers and footers and leave the margins at Default; the page size comes from this layout. Chrome and Edge print the running heads and page numbers. A long selection takes a little while to appear in the print window.
