How to type things
  • Powers: x^2 or x². Roots: sqrt(x), √x, cbrt(x), nthroot(x, 5).
  • Multiplying: 2x, 3(x+1) and x y all multiply; * works too.
  • Constants: pi or π, e, i (√−1), inf (∞).
  • Functions: sin cos tan sec csc cot, asin acos atan, sinh cosh tanh, ln, log (base 10), log(x, b), exp, abs or |x|, floor, ceil, round, n!.
  • On the Graph screen: y = …, x = …, r = …θ… (polar), (x(t), y(t)) (parametric), any equation in x and y, inequalities, points, a = 3 for a slider, f(x) = … for a function, and {-1 < x < 2} at the end to show only part.
  • On the Calculate screen: use := to store: a := 5, f(x) := x^2. A longer name, like scores = {…}, can use =. ans is the last answer. Arrow up brings back what you typed before.
  • Calculus and algebra: d, int, limit, taylor, sum, product, solve, nsolve, roots, factor, expand, simplify, comdenom, tangent.
  • Matrices: [[1,2],[3,4]]; det, inv, transpose, rref, trace, eigenvalues, identity(3), dot, cross, norm.
  • Lists and statistics: {1,2,3}; mean, median, mode, stdev, variance, total, sort, seq, linreg, quadreg, expreg.
  • Numbers: gcd, lcm, mod, isprime, factor, nCr, nPr, hex, bin, oct, real, imag, conj, arg, approx, exact, propfrac, random, randint.
  • The Radians/Degrees button sets how angles are measured. Your work is saved in this browser.

Made for the Teddy Rook Book homeschool pages. Everything runs in your browser, with nothing sent anywhere. It's a learning tool: check anything important with a second method.
