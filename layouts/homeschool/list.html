Homeschool

Old books, taught today

Free, public-domain schoolbooks that families have trusted for generations: reading, writing, spelling, grammar, arithmetic, geometry, science, nature study, history, geography, Latin and Greek. Read them here or download them, then use a step-by-step guide to teach each lesson: what to get out, what to say, and which page to open.

Ray's Arithmetic

The best-known American arithmetic series of the 1800s. All the books to read or download, with guides for every lesson of the Primary, Intellectual, Practical and Higher books and the Elementary Algebra.

McGuffey's Readers

The classic readers, from the Primer's first words to great literature in the Sixth Reader, plus the McGuffey speller. A step-by-step guide for every lesson of all eight books.

Actual Reading, by Hannah Ward

Free modern curricula for the early years, shared by their author: Little House Pre-K and Kindergarten, the alphabet in 60 lessons, learning to read in 120 lessons, American Pioneer for grades 1–3, a phonics boot camp for older learners, and read-aloud lists.

Start with the little ones → ✏️

Geometry

Science

Astronomy, geology, chemistry and physics from the classic science books: Giberne's Sun, Moon, and Stars and The World's Foundations, Faraday's Chemical History of a Candle and Millikan's First Course in Physics, each with a guide for every session and safe things to try. Plus free modern open textbooks.

Start exploring → 🌿

Nature Study

Anna Botsford Comstock's Handbook of Nature-Study, with a guide for each of her 234 lessons: birds, insects, flowers, trees, rocks, weather and stars, taken outdoors and in season. Plus Burgess, Pierson and Fabre to read aloud.

Go outside → 🏺

History

Greece and Rome the way children read them a century ago: Famous Men of Greece, Rome and the Middle Ages with a guide for every chapter, plus Guerber, Macgregor, Church's Homer and Livy, Kingsley's Heroes, Plutarch and more.

Start with the Greeks → 🧭

Geography

Start at home with Long's Home Geography, a guide for every lesson: directions, maps of your own room and street, hills and rivers. Then travel the world with Carpenter's Geographical Readers.

Outdoor Skills

Things to learn and do outdoors and around home: knots, maps and compass, tracks, birds, trees, stars, weather, outdoor cooking, whittling, camping, hiking, gardening and more, inspired by the old handbooks of camping and woodcraft.
