Homeschool

Everything to teach at home, free

One place for the whole school day. The core is classic public-domain schoolbooks, old books taught today: reading, writing, spelling, grammar, arithmetic, geometry, science, nature study, history, geography, Latin and Greek, each with a step-by-step guide that says what to get out, what to say and which page to open. Around them: a lesson planner and placement check, practice sheets, early-years curricula, sign language, outdoor skills, gardening, sewing, Morse code, binary and hex, and puzzles to tie it together. Nothing to sign up for, and nothing to pay.

Math Ladder

Science

Astronomy, geology, chemistry and physics from the classic science books: Giberne's Sun, Moon, and Stars and The World's Foundations, Faraday's Chemical History of a Candle and Millikan's First Course in Physics, each with a guide for every session and safe things to try. Plus free modern open textbooks, and a hands-on physics lesson with a balancing game.

Nature Study
