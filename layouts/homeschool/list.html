Old books, taught today

Free, public-domain schoolbooks that families have trusted for generations: reading, spelling, grammar, arithmetic, geometry, Latin and Greek. Read them here or download them, then use a step-by-step guide to teach each lesson: what to get out, what to say, and which page to open.

Real exams from 1895, 1906 and 1912 to try at home, with answer notes, and a path by age to being ready for them using the books below. A good place to start.
