Homeschool

Everything to teach at home, free

One place for the whole school day. The core is the ladders, full courses from Kindergarten to college with practice that checks every answer: math, writing and grammar, science, history and civics, and geography. Beside them are classic public-domain schoolbooks, each with a step-by-step guide that says what to get out, what to say and which page to open. Around them: a lesson planner and placement check, practice sheets, early-years curricula, sign language, outdoor skills, gardening, sewing, Morse code, binary and hex, and puzzles to tie it together. Nothing to sign up for, and nothing to pay.
