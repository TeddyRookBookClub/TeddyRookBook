Lesson planner

Tell it who you're teaching and how much time you have, and it lays out every lesson, week by week, through the books on this site. Change anything and the plan updates. It's saved in this browser, and you can print it, save it as a PDF or picture, add it to your calendar, or keep a plan file.

1 Your children

For ages 3 to 5 the planner suggests Hannah Ward's Actual Reading curricula (Little House Pre-K and Kindergarten cover math and English together, so Arithmetic and Phonics start switched off), then the classic schoolbooks. Every subject's Book menu lists all the choices.

Under More subjects are the Math Ladder (Kindergarten to college), the Physics, Chemistry, Earth & Space, Economics, Logic, Etiquette and Egyptology Ladders, Music, and life skills from Being an Adult 101 and The Trades. Switch one on and pick a course, or pick “Today's practice” and the ladder chooses each day's lesson or review.
