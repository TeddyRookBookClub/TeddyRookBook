Homeschool · Planner

Lesson planner

Tell it who you're teaching and how much time you have, and it lays out every lesson, week by week, through the ladders and books on this site. Change anything and the plan updates. It's saved in this browser, and you can print it, save it as a PDF or picture, add it to your calendar, or keep a plan file.

1 Your children

The ladders come first. The Math Ladder, Writing & Grammar, American History & Civics and the science ladders (Biology, Earth & Space, Physics and Chemistry) switch on at the ages their courses fit, each with the course for that age. Each child's subjects are listed in the order that matters most at their age, so a six-year-old starts with reading and phonics, and a fifteen-year-old with the Math Ladder and composition.

For ages 3 to 5, Hannah Ward's Actual Reading curricula lead (Little House Pre-K and Kindergarten cover math and English together, so the Math Ladder and phonics start switched off), with McGuffey's Readers for reading. The classic books are all still here: Ray's Arithmetic, Harvey's Grammar, The Mother Tongue, the Palmer Method, Famous Men and the old science books. Pick one from a subject's Book menu or under More subjects.

Teaching several children one theme? Family Study plans a unit for the whole family, with each child's work at their level, and adds it here as a “Family study” subject.

The Science Lab subject gives one play idea a session, a different simulation each time, with the ladder lesson it goes with. In any ladder's Book menu, pick “Today's practice” and the ladder chooses each day's lesson or review.
