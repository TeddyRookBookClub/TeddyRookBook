Hand-skills printables

Sheets for little hands: tracing, mazes, coloring, cutting and dot-to-dot. Pick a kind, check the preview, and print. Press “Generate a new sheet” for a different page every time.

For the grown-up

Tips for little hands

  • Thick crayons, short crayon pieces and chunky pencils are easier to hold.
  • Let your child trace with a finger first, then with a crayon.
  • One sheet, or even one row, is plenty. Stop while it's fun.
  • Put the sheet in a clear sleeve and use a dry-erase marker to trace it again and again.

Hand skills away from the paper

  • Roll, squeeze and pinch play dough.
  • Pick up pom-poms or cereal with clothespins or tongs.
  • Thread big beads or pasta tubes on a shoelace.
  • Draw in a tray of salt or sand with a finger.
  • Tear paper into strips and glue them down.

More printables
