The best free finance & economics tools on the web

Economic data

  • FRED: 800,000+ U.S. and international series from the St. Louis Fed, with charting and downloads.
  • ALFRED: the numbers as first published, before revisions.
  • BLS (jobs, CPI, PPI), BEA (GDP, PCE) and Census (retail sales, housing): the original sources.
  • Atlanta Fed GDPNow and NY Fed Staff Nowcast: real-time GDP estimates.
  • Cleveland Fed Inflation Nowcasting: daily estimates of this month’s CPI and PCE.
  • DBnomics, World Bank, IMF, OECD and Our World in Data: international statistics.

The Fed & interest rates

  • CME FedWatch: market-implied odds of each Fed rate decision.
  • FOMC calendar: meeting dates, statements, minutes and the “dot plot”.
  • TreasuryDirect auctions: announcements and full results; you can also buy Treasuries directly.
  • NY Fed reference rates: SOFR and the fed funds rate each morning.

Charts, screeners & market maps

  • TradingView: the charts on these pages, with alerts and saved layouts on a free account.
  • Finviz: fast screener, market heat maps and insider trades.
  • StockCharts: classic technical charts and market breadth.
  • Koyfin: Bloomberg-style dashboards; the free tier is generous.
  • Yahoo Finance and Google Finance: quotes, news and historical prices.

Company research

  • SEC EDGAR full-text search: every 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K and proxy, searchable.
  • Stock Analysis and Macrotrends: long financial histories and ratios.
  • OpenInsider: insider buying and selling from SEC Form 4.
  • WhaleWisdom: what big funds own (13F filings).
  • Nasdaq earnings calendar: who reports this week.

Valuation & long-run data

  • Multpl: Shiller CAPE, dividend yield and other S&P 500 ratios back to 1871.
  • Aswath Damodaran’s data: equity risk premiums, industry margins and valuation spreadsheets.
  • Kenneth French Data Library: factor returns (value, size, momentum) back to 1926.

Options & volatility

  • Cboe VIX and VIX Central: the index and the full futures curve.
  • OptionStrat and Options Profit Calculator: visualize payoffs for any strategy.

Portfolios & backtesting

  • Portfolio Visualizer: backtest asset allocations, Monte Carlo simulations, factor analysis.
  • Bogleheads wiki: plain-spoken guides to investing and retirement accounts.

Bitcoin

  • mempool.space: live Bitcoin blocks, fees and transactions.
  • The Bitcoin white paper (Satoshi Nakamoto, 2008): nine pages.

Learning

  • Investopedia: definitions of almost every finance term.
  • FRED Blog: short, chart-driven explanations of economic data.
  • Khan Academy: free courses in economics and finance.
