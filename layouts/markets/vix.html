Expected-move calculator

The VIX is the market’s estimate of the S&P 500’s annualized volatility over the next 30 days. Scale by the square root of time: one month ≈ VIX ÷ √12, one day ≈ VIX ÷ √252. About two-thirds of the time the move stays within 1 standard deviation, about 95% within 2. Real markets have fatter tails than this.

VIX futures front month (VX1!) vs. second month (VX2!), delayed

The VIX index itself can’t be traded; futures can. Usually the second month trades above the first (contango): calm now, uncertainty later. When the front month jumps above the second (backwardation), markets are stressed right now. Live index levels are at Cboe, and the full futures curve at VIX Central.
