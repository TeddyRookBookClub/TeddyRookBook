How to use the chart’s analysis tools
  • Indicators (top bar, ƒx): add any of 100+ studies, such as moving averages, Bollinger Bands, RSI, MACD, Stochastic, VWAP, Ichimoku, volume profile, ATR, OBV and more. Click an indicator’s name on the chart to change its settings or remove it.
  • Drawing tools (left toolbar): trend lines, horizontal levels, channels, Fibonacci retracements and extensions, Gann and pitchfork tools, shapes, text notes, and measuring tools for price and date ranges.
  • Compare: type symbols in “Compare with” above, or use the ⊕ button in the chart, to overlay other stocks or ETFs as percentage change.
  • Timeframes: pick the interval above or in the chart. The date-range buttons along the bottom jump to 1 day through all history. Scroll to zoom; drag to pan.
  • Details panel (right side): key stats, a watchlist, and today’s biggest movers. Use the camera icon to save a picture of the chart.
  • Changes you make inside the chart are not saved when you leave the page.
