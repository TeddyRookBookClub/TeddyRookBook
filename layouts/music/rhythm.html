Delay and headphones

Sound takes a moment to reach you, and a touch screen takes a moment to notice a tap. Wired headphones and the device's own speaker add very little. Bluetooth headphones and speakers often add a tenth of a second or more, which makes every tap look late. Measure your delay once with the headphones you use; scores here and on the metronome subtract it.

Keys and touch

  • F or D left hand
  • J or K right hand
  • Space start or stop (Enter presses the button you are on)
  • ↑ ↓ tempo (Shift for five)
  • Esc stop

On a phone or tablet, tap both pads with two fingers or two thumbs; both can be pressed at once. With a MIDI keyboard, keys below middle C are the left hand and middle C and above are the right hand.
