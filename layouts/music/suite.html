Six numbers, six fractals

Where the music came from

The Rhythm of Numbers is the workshop behind the suite: play pi as a beat, see its levels, and compose your own piece from any number. Its levels section shows how a continued fraction turns into bars, phrases and sections, and Compose a piece builds one for you.

New to reading music? Start with Piano Basics and Reading Music, then come back. Keep time with the metronome.
