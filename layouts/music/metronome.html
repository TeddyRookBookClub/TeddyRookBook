Delay and headphones

Sound takes a moment to reach you. Wired headphones and the device's own speaker add very little. Bluetooth headphones and speakers often add a tenth of a second or more, so your taps seem late even when they are right. Measure your delay once with the headphones you use; the metronome and the rhythm trainer both subtract it.

Tempo words

Composers write the speed in Italian words. They describe a mood as much as a speed, so the numbers are approximate: sources differ by a few beats either way. The row for the tempo you have set is highlighted.
