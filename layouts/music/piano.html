Start here

Never sat at a piano? You’re in the right place. Take these four steps in order, a little each day, or jump to whatever you need.

  1. 1Meet the pianoThe black and white keys, finding C, your hands and fingers, and your first notes. No reading yet.
  2. 2Read musicThe staff, the notes, rhythm and key signatures, with short drills to make them stick.
  3. 3Play songsStart with Hot Cross Buns. Every note lights up on the music and on the keys as it sounds.
  4. 4Keep timeA metronome to practice with, and a rhythm trainer for both hands.

Try a key

Press any key. The gold dot marks middle C, the C in the middle of a piano. The Letter names button shows or hides the names of the keys.

On a computer you can also play with the letter keys: A S D F G H J K play C D E F G A B C.
