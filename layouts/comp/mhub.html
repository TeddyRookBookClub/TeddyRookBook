📜 Putnam solutions guide

The William Lowell Putnam Mathematical Competition is a contest for college students in the United States and Canada, held each year in early December: twelve problems in two sessions of three hours. Read a problem at the official link, try it, then come back for a hint or our full solution.

🌍 IMO solutions guide

The International Mathematical Olympiad is the world contest for school students, held each July: six problems over two days, four and a half hours a day. Read a problem at the official link, try it, then come back for a hint or our full solution.
