The Rhythm of Numbers · The paper

The Rhythm of Numbers: Continued Fractions, Primes, Music and Self-Similarity

Teddy Rook · {{ .Params.year }}

Everything on the Rhythm of Numbers pages, written up as one long paper, like a thesis: definitions, theorems and proofs, 40 figures, numbered equations and a full bibliography. It also goes further than the pages, with new computations on prime convergents and on the zeros of the zeta function, and three last chapters of new work on six open questions.

⬇ Download the paper (PDF, {{ .Params.pdf_size }})Open it in a new tab

The paper is written for a strong college student or a curious adult: calculus and a first taste of number theory help.
