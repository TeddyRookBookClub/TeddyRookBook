Across: energy, from −4 to 4. Up: flux from 0 to 1. Each green row is one fraction p/q with q up to 40. Gold rows are this number's own fractions; the dashed line is the number itself.

Total width is the combined length of all bands. It shrinks like 9.33/q, so for an irrational flux nothing solid is left: the allowed energies form a dust, like the Cantor sets of the fractals chapter.

What is known and what is ours. The spectrum is Hofstadter's (1976); the splitting of bands by the continued fraction, and the whole numbers attached to the gaps (they set the measured Hall conductance), are established physics, seen in graphene superlattices in 2013. Reading the levels as the bars, phrases and sections of a number's rhythm is this project's addition.
