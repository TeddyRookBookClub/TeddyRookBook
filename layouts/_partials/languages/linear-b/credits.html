Signs and readings: sign numbers follow Emmett L. Bennett Jr.’s standard list and the Unicode names; sound values, special signs and transliteration follow the conventions in Wikipedia’s articles Linear B, Linear A and Mycenaean Greek, which report the standard reference works (Ventris and Chadwick’s Documents in Mycenaean Greek and Chadwick’s The Decipherment of Linear B). The tripod tablet’s text follows Wikipedia’s PY Ta 641. Linear A values are the conventional Linear B values; they are working guesses, not known sounds.

The fonts: TRB Linear B and TRB Linear A, drawn sign by sign for Teddy Rook Book from the standard sign forms and released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. They sit at the Unicode code points (Linear B Syllabary, Linear B Ideograms, Aegean Numbers and Linear A), so text you copy stays real Unicode.
