Ancient Festival Tycoon

Build festival grounds in ancient Greece or Rome, with theaters, chariot races, baths, bread stalls and temples. Keep your visitors fed and happy, and read what they’re thinking in Greek and Latin.

Imperium

Conquer the ancient world territory by territory, with dice. Fight Pyrrhus for Italy, march with Hannibal, or claim Caesar’s inheritance in Latin; sail against Troy or lead Athens or Sparta in Greek. Choose your rivals, the difficulty and how you start. The Latin and Greek are optional.

Play → 🧩

Mosaic Match

A match-three puzzle with grapes, lamps, ships and swords. Each tile carries its Latin or Koine Greek name, and every match shows the word and its meaning. Thirty levels, plus endless practice.

Play → 🦅

Teutoburg

AD 9. As one of Varus’ legionaries, grab coins and eagle standards through the rain-soaked Teutoburg Forest while the Cherusci, Bructeri, Marsi and Chatti close in. You pick up Latin words as you go, with the real history between rounds.

Play → ⏳

Time Thief

A gang of thieves is hopping through history. Question witnesses in Greek or Latin, follow the clues from Knossos to Patmos or from Romulus to the last emperor, get a warrant and make the arrest, picking up history and vocabulary as you go. 24 cases in each era.
