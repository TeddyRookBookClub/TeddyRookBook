Games

Play in Κοινή & Latina

Games that teach Koine Greek and Latin as you play.

🏛️

Ancient Festival Tycoon

Build festival grounds in ancient Greece or Rome, with theaters, chariot races, baths, bread stalls and temples. Keep your visitors fed and happy, and read what they’re thinking: in Greek if you build in Greece, in Latin if you build in Rome.

Play → 🏺

Hero’s Road

Run and jump through four levels as Theseus in Greece or Hercules in Rome, ending with the Minotaur or the fire-breathing Cacus. Bump the amphora blocks for words, and pick the right door at each gate. Koine Greek with pronunciation, or Latin; words can be turned off.

Play → 🗺️

Imperium

Conquer the ancient world territory by territory, with dice. Fight Pyrrhus for Italy, march with Hannibal, or claim Caesar’s inheritance in Latin; sail against Troy or lead Athens or Sparta in Greek. Choose your rivals, the difficulty and how you start. The Latin and Greek are optional.

Play → 🧩

Mosaic Match

A match-three puzzle with grapes, lamps, ships and swords. Each tile carries its Latin or Koine Greek name, and every match shows the word and its meaning. Thirty levels, plus endless practice.

Play → 🦅

Teutoburg

AD 9. As one of Varus’ legionaries, grab coins and eagle standards through the rain-soaked Teutoburg Forest while the Cherusci, Bructeri, Marsi and Chatti close in. You pick up Latin words as you go, with the real history between rounds.

Play → 🛡️

Thermopylae

480 BC. As Leonidas, hold the pass while the Persian army closes in from every side: turn, throw your spears and break up their troops. Greek only, with a word to learn and the real history after every wave.

Play → ♞

Chess of the Ancients

Chess with ancient armies: Greeks against Trojans with Greek piece names, or Romans against Carthaginians with Latin ones. Turn on the move statistics and every move is ranked and colour-coded by your chances of winning, with the rules beside the board.

Play → 🎲

Tabula

Backgammon, descended from the Roman game of tabula, against the emperor Zeno. Every way to play your roll is ranked and colour-coded, with the pip count and the exact chance of being hit. Rules, Latin words and the history are beside the board.

Play → ⚪

Terni Lapilli

The Roman three-in-a-row that Ovid mentions, still played today as three men’s morris. Place three pebbles, then slide them. The game is fully solved, so the statistics are exact: every move is marked as a win, a draw or a loss.

Play → ⏳

Time Thief

A gang of thieves is hopping through history. Question witnesses in Greek or Latin, follow the clues from Knossos to Patmos or from Romulus to the last emperor, get a warrant and make the arrest, picking up history and vocabulary as you go. 24 cases in each era.

Play →
