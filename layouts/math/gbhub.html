🏡 For families

  • Three paths on every rung. Children of about 8 to 12 take the young readers' path, with a story or an activity; teens and parents take the full lesson; anyone curious can go deeper.
  • Read aloud together. Many of these books were written to be heard. Ten or fifteen minutes after dinner is enough.
  • Talk it over. Each rung ends with questions for the family. There are no wrong opinions, only reasons to give.
  • Let kids climb on their own. The practice is short and checks answers, so older children can work alone and show you their progress.
  • A note for parents appears on rungs with mature parts (war in Homer, some plays, Freud), with suggestions for what to read with younger children.

Start with How to Read a Great Book; it has a sample family week.

📅 Great Books in a Year

Every rung in one year, about 40 XP a day, with the fifteen-minute Harvard Classics reading for each day as an extra. Tracks for young readers, teens and adults, and families reading aloud. Open the one-year path, or climb this ladder at your own pace.
