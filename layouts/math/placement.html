Find your rung

A short check, at most 30 questions, that skips what you already know. Each answer tells us about many topics at once: get one right and the skills under it count as known; say you haven't learned one and the topics built on it wait for later. No hints, no XP, no pressure.

Where should we look?

Pick the course you're in or about to start. The check covers that course and the one before it.

Tips. Use scratch paper. If you've never seen a kind of problem, choose "I haven't learned this" instead of guessing: it makes the result better, not worse. You can stop and redo the check any time, or skip it and pick a course from the ladder.
