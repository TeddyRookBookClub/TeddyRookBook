lede: "To build a clockwork model of the sky you need two wheels whose tooth counts are in the ratio of two periods, say Saturn's year and ours. The true ratio is never a neat fraction, and wheels with millions of teeth cannot be cut. In the 1680s Christiaan Huygens found the way out: the rungs of a continued fraction are the best small pairs."
