how:
  - "**Choose a law** from the buttons, or slide the power yourself. Power 2 is Newton's law; power −1 is a spring."
  - "**Press Play.** The gold dots mark each closest approach. If they pile up on one spot, the orbit closes."
  - "**Read the ladder** under the picture. Each rung is a flower the orbit almost draws: so many petals in so many turns."
  - "**Stretch the orbit** with the second slider. Under Newton's law and under a spring, the orbit closes however stretched it is. Under any other law the step changes."
