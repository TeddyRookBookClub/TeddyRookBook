Music, patterns, math and chemistry look like four separate subjects. Look closer and they keep running into each other. A guitar string and an electron in an atom follow the same rules of standing waves. The colors in a cathedral window come from metal atoms in the glass. A drum, a snowflake and the periodic table all follow patterns that math can describe.

The diagram has one oval for each subject. Every place where ovals overlap is a place where those subjects meet. Pick a region to explore it.
