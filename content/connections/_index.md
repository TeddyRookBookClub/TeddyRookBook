Music, patterns, math, chemistry and physics look like five separate subjects. Look closer and they keep running into each other. A guitar string and an electron in an atom follow the same rules of standing waves. The colors in a cathedral window come from metal atoms in the glass. A planet and an electron obey the same law of orbits. A drum, a snowflake and the periodic table all follow patterns that math can describe.

The diagram has one oval for each subject. Five ovals can overlap in 31 ways, and every one of them is here: each place where ovals overlap is a place where those subjects meet. Physics is the newest oval, added in November 2026 with 16 new regions. Pick a region to explore it.
