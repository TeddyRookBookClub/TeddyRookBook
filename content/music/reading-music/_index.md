Reading music is like reading words: at first you sound out each note, and with practice you see whole groups at a glance. These chapters go in order, and each one builds on the one before. If you haven’t played a piano before, start with [Piano Basics](../piano-basics/) so the keys feel familiar.

The drills are short rounds of ten questions. Do a round or two a day. Your best score at each level is saved in this browser, and a check mark shows each chapter you’ve finished.
