Each chapter takes about ten minutes. Read a little, then try it on the keys: on a real piano or keyboard if you have one, or right here on the screen. You don’t need to read music yet. That comes next, in [Reading Music](../reading-music/).

Your progress stays in this browser, and a check mark shows each chapter you’ve finished.
