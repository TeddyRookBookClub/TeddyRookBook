The course has two parts. **Chapters 1 to 14 are for beginners**: the parts of the ukulele and its story, holding and tuning it, chord charts and tab, your first chords and strums, picking tunes, 3/4 time and the Hawaiian vamp. **Chapters 15 to 24 are intermediate**: the fretboard and scales, minor keys, barre chords, low-G tuning, more strums, fingerpicking, chord melody, ragtime-era style and the great Hawaiian songs.

Each chapter takes about ten minutes. Read a little, then try it: on your own ukulele, or on the diagrams and fretboards here, which play when you press them. Tune up first with the [tuner](../tuner/), and look up any chord in the [chord finder](../chords/).

Your progress stays in this browser, and a check mark shows each chapter you've finished.
