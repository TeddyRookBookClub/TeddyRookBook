Some ideas in math are easiest to meet as pictures. Here you can zoom into a fractal, watch two pendulums part ways, drop a thousand balls through pegs, or play a game show a thousand times. Each piece starts with what you're seeing, then explains why it works, step by step.

**Deep dive: [The Rhythm of Numbers](/connections/rhythm-of-numbers/).** Play pi as a beat, see its continued fraction become musical form, and follow it to the prime numbers through tuning, crystals and factoring.
