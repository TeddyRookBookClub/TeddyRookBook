## About the Gospel of John

John has 21 chapters. It begins with the prologue, “In the beginning was the Word,” and is known for its long conversations and its “I am” sayings. Its Greek is the simplest of the works here, which makes it a good first book after a grammar such as Machen’s.

The small numbers are verses. More about the book: [Grokipedia, Gospel of John](https://grokipedia.com/page/Gospel_of_John).
