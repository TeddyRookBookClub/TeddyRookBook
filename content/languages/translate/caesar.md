## About the Gallic War

Caesar wrote seven books on his campaigns in Gaul from 58 to 52 BC, and his officer Aulus Hirtius added an eighth. Book I is the one students have read first for centuries. Harvard asked for the whole work in 1859 and for Books I–IV by 1880 ([the old Harvard list](/languages/translate/harvard/)).

The numbers in the text are chapter and section: 6.2 is chapter 6, section 2. More about the work: [Grokipedia, *Commentarii de Bello Gallico*](https://grokipedia.com/page/Commentarii_de_Bello_Gallico).
