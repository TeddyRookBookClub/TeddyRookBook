## About the Aeneid

The *Aeneid* has twelve books. Books I–VI follow Aeneas from the fall of Troy, through his stay with Queen Dido at Carthage, to his visit to the underworld. Books VII–XII tell of his war in Italy against Turnus. Harvard’s catalogues of the 1800s asked new students for “the whole of Virgil,” later for *Aeneid* I–VI ([the old Harvard list](/languages/translate/harvard/)).

Read each passage aloud before you translate it. Then go phrase by phrase: find the verb, then its subject, then the rest. More about the poem: [Grokipedia, *Aeneid*](https://grokipedia.com/page/Aeneid).
