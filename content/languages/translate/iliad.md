## About the Iliad

The *Iliad* covers a few weeks near the end of the Trojan War, from Achilles’ anger in Book I to the funeral of Hector in Book XXIV. Harvard asked new students for the first three books, leaving out the Catalogue of Ships in Book II ([the old Harvard list](/languages/translate/harvard/)). Book I alone, 611 lines, is a good first goal.

Read each line aloud: the poem was made to be heard. The numbers are line numbers in each book, so 1.33 is Book 1, line 33. More about the poem: [Grokipedia, *Iliad*](https://grokipedia.com/page/Iliad).
