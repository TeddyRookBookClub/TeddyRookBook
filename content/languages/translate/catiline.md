## About the speech

Cicero gave four speeches against Catiline in November and December of 63 BC. This first one, given in the Senate, is the most famous. Harvard’s 1880 catalogue named Cicero’s *Orations against Catiline* in its Latin exam, and earlier catalogues asked for Cicero’s *Select Orations* ([the old Harvard list](/languages/translate/harvard/)).

The numbers are the speech’s sections, §1 to §33. More about the speeches: [Grokipedia, Catilinarian orations](https://grokipedia.com/page/Catilinarian_orations).
