## About the Anabasis

*Anabasis* means “the march up,” from the coast into Asia. The whole work has seven books; after Cunaxa, the Greeks must fight their way home to the Black Sea. Harvard asked for the whole *Anabasis* in 1859 and for Books I–IV by 1880 ([the old Harvard list](/languages/translate/harvard/)).

The numbers in the text are chapter and section: 2.5 is chapter 2, section 5. More about the work: [Grokipedia, *Anabasis*](https://grokipedia.com/page/Anabasis_(Xenophon)).
